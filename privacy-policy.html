Privacy Policy for Vantage Finance

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Vantage Finance ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal finance tracking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. We wrote this in plain language rather than dense legal text so it's actually useful to read.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in (by email/password or Google Sign-In), we collect your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your name and Google account profile information as provided by Google.

Profile information. Your name and mobile number, if you choose to add them in the app's profile settings.

Financial data you enter. Transactions, balances, categories, card and loan details, and any other financial records you add to the app. This is the core data the app exists to manage.

Scanned documents. If you use the receipt/document scanner feature, the images you scan are processed to extract transaction details. See "Google Gemini API" below for how this data is handled.

Diagnostic and usage data. We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which automatically collect device information (device model, OS version), app usage patterns (screens visited, features used), and crash/error reports if the app malfunctions.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality: tracking your income, expenses, cards, and loans.
  • To sync your data across your devices via cloud backup, if you enable cloud sync.
  • To extract transaction details from scanned receipts using AI.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability and performance.
  • To understand which features are used, so we can prioritize what to build or fix.

We do not sell your personal or financial information to anyone.

Third-Party Services We Use

The app relies on the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — manages sign-in and account security.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google) — stores your profile and transaction backup data if cloud sync is enabled. Access is restricted to your own account only.
  • Google Gemini API — when you use the receipt scanner, the scanned image or extracted text is sent to Google's Gemini API to identify transaction details (amount, merchant, category, date). Google's handling of this data is governed by the Gemini API Terms of Service.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Google) — collects anonymized usage statistics and crash reports to help us improve the app.
  • Google Sign-In / Play Services — used for authentication if you choose to sign in with Google.

We don't control these third parties' own data practices beyond what we send them; we encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy as well.

Where Your Data Lives

Your transactions are stored locally on your device in all cases. If you enable cloud sync, a backup copy is also stored in Google's Cloud Firestore, secured so that only your authenticated account can read or write it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app and keep your account active. You can delete individual transactions or your entire local data at any time within the app. To request deletion of your cloud-synced data and account, contact us using the details below.

Your Choices

  • You can use the app without enabling cloud sync, in which case your data stays only on your device.
  • You can decline to use the receipt scanner feature if you don't want images sent to Google's Gemini API.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Vantage Finance is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
